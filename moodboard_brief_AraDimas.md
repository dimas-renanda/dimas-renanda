Ara & Dimas — Moodboard + Vendor Brief (Classic Emerald + Champagne, Intimate, Siang)

Summary
- Vibe: Classic Elegant — Emerald + Champagne
- Mood: Intimate daytime reception, semi-standing (most guests mingling, dancefloor focal)
- Venue references: /Users/user/.openclaw/media/inbound/8a23ef06-1fe0-4463-836e-46769f6337eb.jpg (ballroom) and /Users/user/.openclaw/media/inbound/f857eee9-861f-4303-8ddf-6ecf0893f430.jpg (drapery entry)

Color Palette (hex + approximate usage)
- Emerald: #0D5B3A — ~30%
- Champagne: #ECDCB0 — ~35%
- Ivory: #F7F3EE — ~25%
- Warm Gold: #C39B61 — ~10%

Key Textures & Materials
- Emerald velvet (sofa, backdrop panels)
- Champagne satin/charmeuse (ceiling drapery, entry drape)
- Polished dark oak (removable dancefloor)
- Crystal (chandeliers, candelabra)
- Gold metal accents (tables, frames)
- White marble tops (cocktail tables)

Floral Direction
- Primary: cream garden roses, peonies
- Accents: ranunculus, anemone white, small spray roses
- Foliage: eucalyptus, pittosporum, dark camellia leaves
- Use low lush arrangements on stage + small clusters on cocktail tables

Lighting
- Daylight‑balanced base (since event siang) + warm 3000K uplights for intimacy
- Three clustered crystal chandeliers above dancefloor (staggered heights)
- Soft stage spotlight and many candle clusters (pillar + votive)
- Optional soft warm gobo texture over dancefloor for subtle patterning

Layout & Zonasi (semi‑standing)
- Stage: use existing stage (1.2–1.5m high). Add emerald velvet full‑height backdrop + gold trim + low floral garland.
- Dancefloor: polished dark oak, ~7m x 6m, directly in front of stage.
- VIP seating: 3 round tables near stage (small dining) with champagne‑brown chair covers.
- Cocktail tables: 12 perimeter white‑marble cocktail tables (gold base) placed around dancefloor.
- Lounge: 4 emerald velvet 2‑seat sofas in two clusters behind dancefloor with gold coffee tables.
- Bar: backlit champagne marble bar on the right wall (2 bartenders during peak).
- Photobooth/welcome: at entrance with soft drapery + neon gold script "Ara & Dimas".
- Walkways: maintain 1.2–1.5m clear path from entrance to stage.

Mandatory for Vendors
- Emerald velvet full‑height backdrop panel
- Removable dark oak dancefloor (7x6 m approximate)
- 3 clustered crystal chandeliers hung above dancefloor
- Warm 3000K ambient uplighting (dimmable) + lighting tech on site
- Champagne‑brown chair covers for 3 VIP tables (replace navy)

Avoid
- Silver metallic accents (use warm gold only)
- Fluorescent blue lighting or harsh blue glares
- Bright pastels that break the palette
- Leaving patterned yellow/gold carpet visually dominant (use drapery/lighting to neutralize)

Install & Timing
- Estimated install: 6–8 hours (drapery rigging, chandeliers, stage dressing, lighting focus)
- Lighting tech required for cues/focus and final color adjustment
